# New-Energy-Vehicle-Market-and-Competitive-Intelligence-System
This project simulates a real-world internship task for an automotive product planning team. The goal is to track brand, model, segment, energy type, price band, and sales volume changes, helping the team identify market structure, competitive pressure, and emerging opportunities. It is the centerpiece of my job-seeking portfolio, directly connecting my vehicle engineering undergraduate background and data science master's training.
